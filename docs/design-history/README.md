| `DESIGN-DIRECTION-V2-proposal.md` | The v2 proposal and its reasoning, with the four approval decisions noted at the top |

**Why Routes replaced v2:** the v2 homepage was trustworthy and well structured, but too restrained and plain for a client-facing product. Two concepts were prototyped against the real data, and Concept A, Routes, was approved: a metro-route identity, wide Anek display type, a navy, cobalt and yellow hierarchy, stream line colours and a departure-board comparison. The temporary design lab used for the exploration has been removed.

**2026-10-11, Pathways, marked up:** after Routes was integrated, the Margin Notes concept (B) was combined with it. Routes stays the identity and structure. Margin Notes adds paper surfaces, a few paper notes, tape and two brief handwritten annotations, under strict restraint rules. The departure-board comparison was replaced by an annotated comparison sheet. The temporary design lab was removed after integration.
